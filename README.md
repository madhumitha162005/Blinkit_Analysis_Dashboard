🛒 Blinkit Analysis Dashboard
📊 Project Overview

The Blinkit Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, outlet characteristics, product categories, customer preferences, and overall business performance.

The dashboard transforms raw retail data into meaningful visual insights using KPIs, charts, filters, and interactive Power BI features.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance.
Track key business KPIs.
Understand product category performance.
Analyze outlet-wise performance.
Identify customer preferences and purchasing patterns.
Compare outlet characteristics such as size and location type.
Generate actionable business insights through interactive visualizations.

📊 Dashboard Analysis

Analyzes overall sales and average sales to understand business performance.

📦 Product Analysis

🏪 Outlet Analysis

Examines average customer ratings to understand customer experience and product performance.
